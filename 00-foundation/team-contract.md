## How we work
- **Channel:** IRL, or by group chat
- **Standup:** On the weekends
- **Response window:** messages acknowledged within 1-2 hours, any time of the day
- **Silent teammate with a deadline near:** Do a wellness check; team stays on contact at all times.
- **Demo-ready:** any member can screen-share the current product state in any lab, on 2 minutes notice

## How we decide
Small stuff: any two members. Direction: evidence first, then majority; the dissent gets one sentence in the record. From Week 3, product decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` with a link to their evidence. That file is the team memory; keep it honest.

## When it breaks
1. Name it in the team channel within 48 hours of noticing. Silence is how last year's teams collapsed.
2. Still stuck after one standup: raise it at a lab checkpoint.
3. Involves everyone, or step 2 failed: email zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge for mediation.
