**Context:** 34 year-old administrative coordinator at a university dean's office. Processes student tuition payment confirmations, scholarship applications, and faculty expense documents.

**Cost:** ~1 full work week every semester spent reconciling student receipts and digging through archive boxes. delayed student registrations when proof cannot be matched quickly.
**Frequency:** Peak chaos twice a year at semester start, plus daily receipt handling during registration weeks.
**Also reported:** Students frequently send cropped mobile banking screenshots missing the transaction ID or payer name.
**Prediction check:** raised on her own when describing registration week bottlenecks.
**Surprise ★:** Even when documents are digitized via office scanners, auto-generated names  make digital retrieval almost as slow as physical boxes.
