# Interview 03 · Daily commuter to Tbilisi · (code name: Transit-1)

**Week:** 1 · **Asker:** GG · **Logger:** GG (written from memory; no second logger) · **Consent:** yes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** A resident of Rustavi who travels to Tbilisi every morning for office work. Interviewed at a local cafe.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ბილეთის ფასი აღარც მაინტერესებს, გაყინულზე დაგვიანებული მარშრუტკის ლოდინი ნიშნავს, რომ ადგილას უკვე დაღლილი მივდივარ სანამ დღე დაიწყება." (I don't even care about the ticket price anymore; standing in the cold waiting for a delayed bus means I arrive at my destination already exhausted before my trip even starts.)
- "სამუშაო დღეს თითქმის ყოველთვის თავის ტკივილით ვიწყებ, რადგან საცობების არაპროგნოზირებადობა ლეპტოპის გახსნამდე სტრესის პიკზე მაყენებს." (I start my workday with a headache almost every time because the unpredictability of the traffic jams keeps my stress levels at a maximum before I even open my laptop.) ★
- "ყველაზე ცუდი მარშრუტკაში ის არის, როცა შუაში დამატებით დასაკეც სკამს შლიან და ერთი საათი ფეხს ვერ ანძრევ." (The absolute worst part of the marshrutka is when they cram an extra foldable seat in the aisle and you can't move your legs for an hour.)

**Workaround:** leaves the house way too early to guarantee a spot; endures extreme physical discomfort during the ride.
**Cost:** arriving at the destination completely exhausted and highly stressed; starting the workday with a headache.
**Frequency:** 5 days a week.
**Prediction check:** True. They brought up the cost of exhaustion and stress before I even asked about how it affects their workday.
**Surprise ★:**  I thought the biggest complaint would be the price of the commute or transit duration, but the physical stress of waiting and unpredictability completely overshadowed the financial cost.
**Commitment:** agreed to a follow-up chat to map out their morning routine step-by-step.
**Next:** interview someone who specifically travels intercity for university, to see if student constraints differ from office workers.
