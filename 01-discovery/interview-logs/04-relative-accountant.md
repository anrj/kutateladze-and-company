# Interview 04 · Small business accountant managing expense documents and invoices · (code name: Accountant-1)

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** DK · **Logger:** GG · **Consent:** yes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 36-year-old accountant managing bookkeeping and tax declarations for two small retail and service companies in Tbilisi. Interviewed by Davit about how she collects primary expense documents from employees and reconciles accounts before monthly tax deadlines.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "თვის ბოლოს დეკლარაცია რომ მაქვს გასაგზავნი, თანამშრომლებს სათითაოდ ვეხვეწები WhatsApp-ში ჩეკების ფოტოები გამომიგზავნეთ-მეთქი. ნახევარი დაკარგულია, ნახევარი დაბურული ფოტოა და ციფრები არ ჩანს" / "When I have to submit tax declarations at the end of the month, I have to beg employees one by one on WhatsApp to send photos of expense receipts. Half are lost, half are blurry photos where numbers aren't visible."
- "Google Drive-ზე მაქვს საქაღალდეები გაკეთებული თვეების მიხედვით, მაგრამ მაინც ქაოსია — ფაილებს არქმევენ 'IMG_2041'-ს და მერე საათობით ვეძებ რომელი ჩეკი რომელ ზედნადებს ეკუთვნის" / "I made folders on Google Drive broken down by month, but it's still chaos — people name files 'IMG_2041' and then I spend hours searching which receipt belongs to which invoice."
- "★ შარშან საგადასახადომ შეგვამოწმა და პირველადი დოკუმენტი რომ ვერ წარვადგინეთ ერთ ხარჯზე, 500 ლარით დაგვაჯარიმეს. არადა ვიცოდით რომ გადახდილი იყო, უბრალოდ ფიზიკური ჩეკი გაქრა" / "★ Last year the Revenue Service audited us and because we couldn't produce the original primary document for one expense, they fined us 500 GEL. We knew it was paid, but the physical slip simply vanished."

**Workaround:** a shared Google Drive with folders structured by year and month where staff upload mobile scans, combined with a physical ring binder in the office where paper originals are supposed to be filed
**Cost:** 500 GEL tax penalty for an unsubstantiated expense claim during a Revenue Service check; 6 to 8 hours wasted every month chasing missing slips and manually renaming image files
**Frequency:** acute crunch occurs once a month before the 15th tax declaration deadline; receipt logging happens weekly
**Also reported by the accountant:** staff regularly throw away small cash receipts for office supplies or fuel after swiping company cards, not realizing bank statements alone do not satisfy tax audit requirements
**Prediction check:** raised on their own before we mentioned it
**Surprise ★:** the company was penalized even though bank records proved the payment went through, solely because the physical paper source document could not be produced
**Commitment:** shared screen to show the messy Google Drive directory with unindexed photos; asked to test any tool that can auto-extract dates and amounts from photos
**Next:** Interview a small company director or operations lead to check whether they track how many billable accounting hours are lost on missing paper receipts.
