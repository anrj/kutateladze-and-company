# Interview 01 · Corner-store seller, neighbourhood shop that sells on credit ("nisia") · (code name: Shop-1)

**Week:** 4 · **Asker:** AJ · **Logger:** NT · **Consent:** yes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Seller at a small corner store that gives regular customers goods on credit (nisia). Interviewed by Anri about how they track nisia and whether a ledger would help.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "აბა შვილო რვეულში ვიწერ და რო ვანახებ ხოლმე მაინც მეუბნებია ცდებიო და რა ვუყო" / "Well, child, I write it in the notebook, and when I show them, they still tell me I'm mistaken, and what am I supposed to do?"
- "ნისიებს აღარ ვიძლევი თუ ნაცნობი არაა მართლა, თორე დავიღალე აღარ შემიძლია" / "I no longer give nisia unless it's someone I really know. I'm tired, I can't do it anymore."
- "...უარი როგორ ვუთხრა განა, სადარბაზოში როგორ ვუთხრა გამარჯობა მერე" / "How could I refuse them? How would I even say hello to them afterwards in the building entrance?"

**Workaround:** a notebook where she writes each customer's name, the date, the amount and the products taken; she shows it to customers who dispute amounts
**Cost:** repeat non-payers, neighbours she cannot refuse, and some customers who never came back; customers dispute her notebook entries even when she shows them the page
**Frequency:** not frequent anymore; she now gives nisia only to people she knows well
**Also reported by the seller:** she does not have a computer, and neither do the other shopkeepers she knows.
**Prediction check:** only after we asked
**Surprise ★:** none
**Commitment:** nothing
**Next:** She did not name a specific person to talk to; she only implied that other shops have the same problem. Ask the next shopkeeper how they decide who gets nisia.
