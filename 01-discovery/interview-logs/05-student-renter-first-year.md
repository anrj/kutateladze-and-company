# Interview 03 · First-year student from Kakheti, renting a shared apartment near the university in Tbilisi · (code name: Renter-1)

**Week:** 5 · **Asker:** NT · **Logger:** AJ · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** First-year student who moved to Tbilisi from a town in Kakheti in September. Shares a two-room apartment near the university with one other girl. Found the place in the last week of August.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ფეისბუქის ჯგუფებში ვიჯექი დილიდან საღამომდე, ოცდაათზე მეტ ნომერზე დავრეკე და ნახევარი უკვე გაქირავებული იყო." / "I sat in the Facebook groups from morning till night, I called more than thirty numbers and half were already rented."
- "მეპატრონე მეგონა და აღმოჩნდა მაკლერი, ნახევარი თვის ქირა გადავუხადე უბრალოდ რომ კარი გაგვიღო." / "I thought he was the owner and he turned out to be a broker; I paid him half a month's rent just for opening the door for us."
- "დედაჩემი ვიდეოზე არ ენდობოდა, ორჯერ ჩამოვედით თელავიდან ბინის სანახავად." / "My mother didn't trust it over video; we came from Telavi twice just to see apartments." ★
- "ფოტოებზე სხვა ბინა იყო, მივედით და სარემონტო იყო." / "The photos were of a different apartment; we got there and it needed renovation."

**Workaround:** scrolled 4–5 Facebook groups and one listing site every day, called numbers in order, asked relatives in Tbilisi to check places in person when she couldn't come
**Cost:** about 3 weeks of searching; broker fee of 250 GEL (half of the 500 GEL monthly rent); two round trips Telavi–Tbilisi with her mother (her estimate: around 60 GEL, plus two lost work days for the mother)
**Frequency:** one intense search before the semester; she expects to do it again next summer because her flatmate is graduating
**Prediction check:** yes; she raised outdated posts and the broker fee herself when describing the hardest part, before we mentioned either
**Surprise ★:** the parent's trust was a bigger blocker than finding listings; the family would not pay a deposit for a place no one they knew had seen in person
**Commitment:** agreed to a follow-up; offered to introduce her flatmate, who found her roommate through the same groups
**Next:** talk to the flatmate; ask the next student who decided whether a place was safe to pay for, and what they needed to see before paying
