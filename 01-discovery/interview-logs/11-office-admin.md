# Interview 11 · Office administrator managing utilities, contracts, and vendor receipts · (code name: OfficeAdmin-1)

**Week:** 5 · **Asker:** GG · **Logger:** DK (written from memory; no second logger) · **Consent:** yes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 38-year-old office administrator at a 15-person private clinic in Kutaisi. Handles utility bills, rent contracts, courier deliveries, and equipment warranties for the office.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the interviewee does not raise document retrieval failures or their cost on their own before we mention them.

**Workaround:** Physical lever-arch folders in an office cabinet for paper contracts, plus searching across Gmail, Messenger, and WhatsApp when digital bills arrive.
**Cost:** 180 GEL lost on a rejected printer warranty due to faded thermal receipt; ~3–4 hours per month digging through cabinets and messaging vendors for duplicate invoices.
**Frequency:** Documents arrive daily, urgent retrieval requests happen 2–3 times a month.
**Also reported:** Even when she takes a quick phone photo of a receipt, she never renames it, so finding it months later requires scrolling through thousands of gallery photos.
**Prediction check:** raised on their own when asked about the most frustrating part of office administration.
**Surprise ★:** Even carefully filed physical paper receipts become useless because thermal ink fades completely before the warranty period ends.
**Commitment:** will introduce us to other office administrator.
