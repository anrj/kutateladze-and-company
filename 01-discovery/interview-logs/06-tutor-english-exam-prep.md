# Interview 06 · English and IELTS tutor, about 14 students a week · (code name: Tutor-1)

**Week:** 5 · **Asker:** NT · **Logger:** DK · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Teaches English and IELTS prep from home and online, mostly school students whose parents pay, plus a few adults. Has been tutoring for six years. Introduced by a classmate whose sister studies with her.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ყველაფერი ვოთსაპშია, თითო მშობელთან ცალკე ჩატი, ზოგთან ბავშვთანაც ცალკე." / "Everything is on WhatsApp, a separate chat with every parent, and with some of them a separate one with the kid too."
- "გაკვეთილამდე ერთი საათით ადრე მწერენ, დღეს ვერ მოვალთო, და ის საათი უბრალოდ იკარგება." / "They write 'we can't come today' an hour before the lesson, and that hour is just lost." ★
- "გადახდას რვეულში ვინიშნავ, მაგრამ ხანდახან მავიწყდება ჩაწერა და მერე ვერ ვიხსენებ ვინ გადაიხადა." / "I note payments in a notebook, but sometimes I forget to write it down and then I can't remember who paid."
- "მშობელმა მითხრა ოთხი გაკვეთილი იყო და არა ხუთიო, ვერ დავუმტკიცე და დავთმე." / "A parent told me it was four lessons, not five; I couldn't prove it, so I let it go."
- "გაუქმებაზე ფულს არ ვიღებ, უხერხულია." / "I don't charge for cancellations, it's awkward."

**Workaround:** one WhatsApp chat per family; a paper notebook with the week's schedule and payment ticks; asks parents to pay at the end of each month in cash or by bank transfer
**Cost:** 2–4 last-minute cancellations a week at 40 GEL per lesson (her estimate: 80–160 GEL a week lost); one disputed lesson last month (40 GEL, written off); "an hour or more every evening" answering messages and moving lessons
**Frequency:** cancellations every week; payment confusion "a few times a semester"
**Prediction check:** yes; she raised cancellations and the time spent on WhatsApp herself when we asked about the hardest part of her week
**Surprise ★:** the biggest money loss is cancellations, not unpaid lessons, and she refuses to charge for them because she is afraid of losing the family
**Commitment:** agreed we can come back with something to show; offered to introduce one other tutor (math, school students) and one parent (see interview 08)
**Next:** ask the math tutor about the last cancellation and whether they charge for it; ask a parent how a cancellation looks from their side
