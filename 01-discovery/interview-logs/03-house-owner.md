# Interview 01 · Homeowner managing family household warranties and documents · (code name: Homeowner-1)

**Week:** 1 · **Asker:** DK · **Logger:** GL · **Consent:** yes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Homeowner and family member (uncle, 48) who handles home maintenance, electronics, renovation contracts, and appliance purchases for a family of four. Interviewed by Davit about how the family keeps track of warranties and bills, and what happened the last time something broke in the house.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ცენტრალური გათბობის ქვაბი რომ გაითიშა შუა ზამთარში, ვიცოდი რომ საბუთი სადღაც მქონდა, მაგრამ ვერსად ვერ ვიპოვეთ. ბავშვები იყინებოდნენ, ამიტომ გამოვიძახეთ სხვა ხელოსანი და 220 ლარი გადავუხადეთ უცებ" / "When the central heating boiler shut down in the middle of winter, I knew I had the document somewhere, but we couldn't find it anywhere. So I lost out twice just because of a lost piece of paper."

**Workaround:** a plastic transparent folder in the hallway cabinet mixed with gas and electricity receipts, though family members often leave receipts in pockets or kitchen counters instead
**Cost:** 220 GEL paid out of pocket for boiler repairs and parts that were still covered under official warranty; half a day spent stressed searching drawers
**Frequency:** acute search happens once or twice a year during household emergencies; paper receipts enter the house almost every week
**Also reported by the homeowner:** he doesn't know which warranties are still active and which expired, so old useless papers pile up for years because he is afraid to throw them away
**Prediction check:** raised on their own before we mentioned it
**Surprise ★:** the private repairman pointed out the issue was a free warranty defect, which made the financial loss from the missing document feel much more painful
**Commitment:** opened the hallway drawer immediately to show the messy pile of mixed papers; asked Davit to send him whatever solution we come up with
**Next:** He mentioned his wife buys small kitchen appliances online. Ask her how she keeps track of digital receipts versus paper slips delivered by couriers.
