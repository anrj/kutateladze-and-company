## A message that gets a yes (adapt, do not copy)
> Hi [name], I am doing a university project about how [their world, e.g. guesthouse bookings] actually works day to day. Not selling anything, no idea to pitch. Could I ask you about it for 20 minutes this week? You would genuinely be helping me.
Notes: name their world, not your idea. Ask for 20 minutes, not "a chat." Give two time options in the follow-up.
