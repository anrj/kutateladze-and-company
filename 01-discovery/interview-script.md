# Interview Script v1

**Problem we are investigating:** how neighbourhood shop owners track and collect customer credit ("nisia")
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.
**Asker:** Anri · **Logger:** Nikoloz · **Language:** Georgian (English beside it in the repo)

## Opening (2 min, word for word)
"გამარჯობათ, მე ანრი ვარ, KIU-ს სტუდენტი. ვსწავლობთ, როგორ მუშაობს პატარა მაღაზიები ყოველდღიურად. არაფერს გყიდით და არაფერი გვაქვს საჩვენებელი. შეიძლება ჩემმა გუნდელმა ჩანაწერები გააკეთოს? თქვენს სახელს არსად გამოვიყენებთ."
"Hello, I'm Anri, a student at KIU. We're learning how small shops work day to day. I'm not selling anything and I don't have anything to show you. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We won't use your name anywhere."

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. Tell me about your [work or situation]. How did you get started?

## Core (12 to 15 min)
2. Walk me through the last time [the problem moment] happened, from start to finish.
3. Tell me about the last time it went wrong.
4. What did that cost you? *(push for a number)*
5. How do you deal with it today? *(then: can you show me?)*
6. Tell me about the last time [situation for a belief we did not pick]. *(one past-tense question per unpicked belief; do not name the belief)*

## Close (2 min)
7. Who else should we talk to about this?
8. Could we come back when we have something to show you?

## Follow-up bank (glance here when stuck)
- And then what happened? · Why was that a problem? · When was the last time that happened? *(after "usually" or "always")*
- Can you show me? · What would that let you do that you can't now? *(after a feature request)* · Silence: count to three.
