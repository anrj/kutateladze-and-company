## Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** the people who handle a recurring task by hand in our pooled problems (for example shop owners, farmers and students)
> **struggle with** the extra effort and loss that task causes them
> **when** the task has to be done, or goes wrong, in their normal day,
> **which costs them** time, money or something they wanted to keep (produce, learning, trust),
> **Today they** cope with a manual workaround, such as a notebook, their own effort or a favour from someone,
> **We are wrong if** the people we interview do not raise the problem or its cost on their own, when they describe the hardest part of the task, before we mention it.
