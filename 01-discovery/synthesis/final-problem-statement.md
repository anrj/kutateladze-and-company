## Problem Statement

Household document keepers and small-organization administrators in Georgia waste 2 to 8 hours per month and lose 140 to 500+ GEL per incident because incoming paper and digital proof scatter across disconnected drawers, chats, emails, and unindexed folders without searchable metadata during urgent searches, leading to out-of-pocket repeat repairs, tax penalties, duplicate payments, and high-stress compliance scrambles.

---

## Problem Statement Components

### WHO (User Segment)
**Specific user segment we're targeting:**  
Adults responsible for keeping the operational, financial, or warranty documents that a Georgian household or small organization (under ~20 staff) depends on-specifically household document keepers (parents/homeowners) and small-business/office administrators or accountants.

**Why this segment:**  
Unlike students with few recurring obligations, these keepers receive paper and digital slips weekly, face external deadlines or emergencies (tax audits, appliance failures, inspections), and are easily reachable through family, small businesses, and university admin offices.

### WHAT (Core Problem/Failure)
**Specific problem or failure:**  
They fail to capture and retrieve valid, readable primary proof within the short time window required when an unexpected failure, dispute, or deadline strikes-even when they already maintain folders, binders, or Google Drive directories.

**How we validated this:**  
8/15 interviews (`03, 04, 06, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15`) reported multi-channel fragmentation and time-consuming search failures (`8/9` relevant document/record logs).

### HOW MUCH (Quantified Impact)
**Time impact:**  
20–40 minutes per ad-hoc search (`11, 13`), half a day during household emergencies (`03`), 2 hours × 5 staff during inspections (`15`), 6–8 hours every month chasing and renaming receipts (`04`), up to 1 full work week per semester (`14`).

**Financial impact:**  
140 GEL stroller part (`13`), 180 GEL rejected printer warranty (`11`), 220 GEL out-of-pocket boiler repair (`03`), 350 GEL duplicate supplier invoice payment (`12`), 500 GEL Revenue Service tax fine (`04`), and ~1,400 GEL/month in unsubstantiated company card expenses (`12`).

**Emotional / operational impact:**  
Acute panic and stress ("The kids were freezing, so we just called another repairman", "it's still chaos", "I have to beg employees one by one", "eventually I just give up") plus formal regulatory warnings (`15`).

**How we quantified this:**  
Direct financial figures, time estimates, and failure stories reported directly by interviewees in discovery logs `03`–`15`.

### WHY (Root Cause)
**Root cause (not symptom):**  
Documents are generated and collected across disconnected channels (couriers, cash registers, WhatsApp, email, SMS) by multiple people at busy moments, while existing folders and binders require manual sorting, naming, and status tracking-so files sit unnamed (`IMG_2041`, `scan_001`) or fade on thermal paper until a rare crisis hits.

**How we determined root cause:**  
5 Whys root cause analysis of Pattern #1 (`Fragmented Storage Across People, Channels, and Devices`) and Pattern #2 (`Deadline Crises & Time Wasted Searching or Chasing`) in `01-discovery/synthesis/patterns-analysis.md`.

### WHEN/WHERE (Context)
**Context in which problem occurs:**  
Capture fails daily/weekly when receipts arrive in drawers, pockets, WhatsApp chats, or emails; the acute failure surfaces during urgent appliance breakdowns (`03`), warranty claims (`11, 13`), monthly tax filings before the 15th (`04, 12`), semester registrations (`14`), or unannounced regulatory inspections (`15`).

**Frequency:**  
Documents enter weekly/daily; acute retrieval crises occur monthly (`04, 11, 12`), seasonally/semesterly (`14`), or 1–2 times per year (`03, 15`).

### CONSEQUENCES (Real Impact)
**Operational / compliance consequences:**  
Tax penalties (`04`), formal regulatory warnings (`15`), delayed student registrations (`14`), and inability to deduct legitimate expenses (`12`).

**Time consequences:**  
2 to 8 hours per month (and up to a full week per semester) spent chasing coworkers, digging through archive boxes, and opening files one by one.

**Emotional consequences:**  
Household friction over who threw a paper away (`03`), exhaustion from nagging employees (`04`), and helplessness when paying twice for something already covered (`03, 12`).

**Financial consequences:**  
Direct losses of 140–500+ GEL per incident from rejected warranties, repeat payments, and missing primary tax documents.

**Other consequences:**  
Fear of discarding valid paperwork causes years of expired clutter to accumulate in drawers and binders (`03, 15`).

## Decision Documentation

### Original Problem Statement (Week 2)

**Our original problem statement was (`00-foundation/team-problem-statement.md`):**
> "Adults who are the keepers of the paper a household or a small company depends on, such as warranty cards, receipts and expense slips, like a 48-year-old homeowner in a family of four and a 36-year-old accountant for two small companies in Tbilisi (03, 04), face the problem on the day one specific paper suddenly matters-when the boiler fails in winter or tax declarations are due-costing them 220 GEL + half a day searching (03) and a 500 GEL tax fine + 6–8 hours/month chasing slips (04), because documents arrive constantly and are filed without thought of the rare, urgent moment when one becomes the only proof."

### Decision: ☐ KEEP  ☒ REFINE  ☐ PIVOT

**We decided to REFINE because:**

Our core hypothesis from Week 2-that adults keeping household and small-company documents lose significant money and time when rare emergencies or audits expose poor filing-was strongly reinforced rather than invalidated. Our logs confirmed that the pain does not stem from lacking a folder, since users already maintain plastic folders, binders, WhatsApp chats, and Google Drive.

However, synthesis revealed that the root cause is specifically **multi-channel, multi-person fragmentation plus unindexed/degrading records** (`IMG_2041`, `scan_001`, compressed WhatsApp photos, fading thermal ink), not merely "filing without thought of rare emergencies." Refining the statement also tightened our quantified impact range (`2–8 hours/month`, `140–500+ GEL/incident`), expanded context beyond boilers and monthly taxes to include regulatory inspections, university registration reconciliation, and courier/online warranty claims, and explicitly separated document keepers from informal manual-ledger disputes (`01, 06`).

### What Changed from Original to Final

| Component | Original (Week 2) | Final (Week 5) | Why It Changed |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|----------------|
| WHO | 48yo homeowner & 36yo accountant (`03, 04`) | Household document keepers & small-organization administrators (`03, 04, 11–15`) | Broadened cleanly to include office admins, uni coordinators, and small retail/company leads from `icp.md` |
| WHAT | Losing a specific paper when it suddenly matters | Failing to retrieve legible, indexed primary proof across fragmented channels and unindexed files | Synthesis showed digital folders (`IMG_2041`, `scan_001`) and WhatsApp chats fail just as badly as paper drawers |
| HOW MUCH | 220–500 GEL; 0.5 day to 6–8 hrs/month | 140–500+ GEL/incident (up to 1,400 GEL/mo unverified expenses); 2–8 hrs/month (up to 1 wk/semester) | Added ranges from `11` (180 GEL), `12` (350 GEL duplicate, 1,400 GEL unverified), `13` (140 GEL), `14`, `15` |
| WHY | Filed without thought of rare urgent moments | Multi-person, multi-channel capture lacks automatic indexing/legibility preservation at arrival time | 5 Whys on Pattern #1 revealed manual renaming/sorting friction is why shared Drive/binders degrade |
| WHEN/WHERE | Boiler fails in winter; monthly tax declaration | Urgent repairs, warranty claims, monthly tax deadlines, semester registrations, and regulatory inspections | Real logs highlighted retail inspections (`15`) and semester reconciliations (`14`) |
| CONSEQUENCES | Paid repair, tax fine, hours searching | Out-of-pocket repairs, tax fines, duplicate supplier payments, formal warnings, and binder bloat | Added duplicate invoice payments (`12`), thermal warranty rejections (`11`), and regulatory warnings (`15`) |

### Key Learnings from Interviews

1. **Insight 1 - Users already have storage places, so "another folder" won't help:**
    - **Evidence:** `03` (hallway folder), `04` (monthly Google Drive + ring binder), `14` (shared scan folder + archive boxes), `15` (binders).
    - **Impact on problem statement:** Shifted our root cause from "no storage" to "fragmented capture + unindexed files (`IMG_2041`, `scan_001`)."
2. **Insight 2 - Physical paper and informal chats actively destroy proof:**
    - **Evidence:** `11` (thermal ink fades in 6 months), `04` (bank statements fail tax audits without the primary slip), `13` (WhatsApp compresses and buries lab/warranty photos).
    - **Impact on problem statement:** Emphasized legible, indexed *primary proof* preservation rather than casual photo backups.
3. **Insight 3 - Capture habits already exist inside messaging apps, just without structure:**
    - **Evidence:** `04` (staff WhatsApp receipts), `12` (drivers texting photos), `13` (wife sending clinic/receipts to husband's WhatsApp).
    - **Impact on problem statement:** Anchored WHO/WHY in multi-person capture workflows where non-admin senders take the easiest path available.

## Open Questions & Uncertainties

### What We Still Don't Know

**Question 1:** What makes a spouse, driver, or employee actually send/save a receipt the instant they receive it?
- **Why it matters:** If non-admin contributors refuse to use a separate app, any centralized system will still suffer from missing slips (`04, 12`).
- **How we'll address it:** Prototype-test low-friction capture paths (e.g., forwarding inside existing chat vs. 1-tap camera upload) during Week 6 usability sessions.

**Question 2:** Will households retain long-term engagement if acute document emergencies only happen 1–2 times a year (`03`)?
- **Why it matters:** Small-business admins (`04, 11, 15`) experience weekly/monthly pull, whereas households may churn between rare emergencies.
- **How we'll address it:** Compare retention expectations across household vs. small-office early adopters during Week 6–8 MVP rollout.

### Risks & Concerns

**Risk 1:** Interviews overstate willingness to organize household papers consistently.
- **Likelihood:** Low
- **Impact if true:** High (low weekly retention for household users)
- **Mitigation plan:** Prioritize more small-business/office admins (`04, 11, 12, 15`) who face weekly/monthly tax & compliance volume alongside households, and validate with them.

**Risk 2:** Legal tax audits (`04`) may still require physical paper originals in Georgia, limiting what digital copies alone can replace.
- **Likelihood:** Medium
- **Impact if true:** Medium
- **Mitigation plan:** Clarify Georgian Revenue Service primary-document rules with `04` (Accountant-1) and focus on searchability + physical folder location tagging + instant digital backup before thermal ink fades.

### Areas Requiring Further Investigation

- [ ] Ask interviewees directly what they were thinking at the moment they misfiled a slip
- [ ] Confirm Georgian Revenue Service digital vs. paper original requirements with `04`
- [ ] Test whether households vs. small-office admins show stronger weekly retention in Week 6–8

## Problem Scope & Boundaries

### In Scope
1. **User segment:** Georgian household document keepers and small-organization admins/accountants (`< 20` staff).
2. **Context:** Capture, indexing, and urgent retrieval of warranties, expense receipts, invoices, utility/clinic slips, and compliance certificates.
3. **Core problem:** Multi-channel fragmentation and unindexed files (`IMG_2041`, `scan_001`) causing slow/failed retrieval under deadline pressure.
4. **Impact addressed:** Search hours wasted and direct financial losses from lost proof or faded thermal slips.

### Out of Scope
1. **Excluded segment:** Neighborhood corner stores without computers or smartphones (`01`), and students with minimal recurring paperwork (`icp.md`).
2. **Excluded context:** Full double-entry accounting/ERP payroll systems (`12` confirmed staff won't adopt heavy ERP).
3. **Related but separate problems:** Informal social credit (*nisia*, `01`), private tutoring cancellation enforcement (`06`), and unrelated Week 1–5 topics (`02, 05, 07–10`).

### Adjacent Problems (Future Iterations)
1. **Pattern #5:** Automated warranty, calibration, and insurance expiration reminders (`03, 11, 15`).
2. **Audit & Claim Packs:** One-click monthly tax reconciliation or warranty claim export bundles (`04, 13`).

## Success Criteria

### How We'll Know We've Solved This Problem

**Quantitative Success Metrics:**
1. **Retrieval Time:** Reduce time to locate a specific warranty, receipt, or contract from `20 min – 2 hrs` down to `< 15 seconds`.
2. **Indexed Capture Rate:** `≥ 90%` of captured documents stored with searchable vendor, date, and amount (zero `IMG_2041` / `scan_001` dead files).
3. **Monthly Admin Chasing Time:** Reduce accountant/admin reconciliation chasing time (`6–8 hrs/mo` in `04`) by `≥ 70%`.

**Qualitative Success Indicators:**
1. Document keepers report confidence knowing where active warranties and receipts live without keeping shoeboxes/drawers of expired clutter.
2. Users stop sending receipts to personal spouse/employee WhatsApp chats as a makeshift archive.
3. Early adopters invite a spouse, coworker, or accountant into their shared space without prompting.

### Desired Outcomes

**For users:**
- Produce valid warranty or expense proof in seconds during emergencies, returns, or audits-avoiding 140–500+ GEL surprise losses.
- Eliminate evening/month-end anxiety digging through drawers, binders, and chat histories.

**For stakeholders (accountants, managers, inspectors):**
- Receive legible, named, date-stamped primary documents automatically instead of blurry month-end WhatsApp dumps.

## Strategic Implications

### Why This Problem Matters

**Impact scale:**  
Applies to virtually every Georgian household managing home appliances/medical bills and every small retail, service, or office business subject to tax declarations and compliance checks.

**Impact severity:**  
Moderate day-to-day annoyance that escalates into severe financial shocks (140–500+ GEL per missed slip, regulatory warnings, or duplicate payments) at the worst possible moments.

**Market opportunity:**  
Small-office admins and accountants face recurring monthly pain (`04, 11, 12, 15`), creating a realistic path to B2B/prosumer SaaS value once household capture proves sticky.

### Problem vs. Solution

**This statement is about the PROBLEM, not the solution.**

**Premature solution ideas we're avoiding:**
- **Building a full Georgian tax-filing / ERP suite** - Overbuilt for an MVP and rejected by field staff (`12`).
- **Adding AI chatbots or complex folder hierarchies** - Users already fail inside Drive folder trees (`04`); adding manual configuration won't fix capture.
- **Hardware document scanners** - Office scanners still output `scan_001.pdf` (`14`); the problem is indexing and workflow, not scanner hardware.

**We're staying problem-focused because:**  
Locking into a specific UI or feature before Week 6 risks building another passive folder that spouses and employees ignore-the exact failure mode we observed across our interviews.
