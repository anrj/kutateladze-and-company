## Pattern 1

**Pattern (a sentence that says what the evidence shows, not a one-word topic):** A missing paper document costs people real money, even when the payment or the right behind it was never in doubt.

**Interviews behind it (2 minimum):** 03, 04

**Quotes (verbatim, each tagged with its log number):**
- "When the central heating boiler shut down in the middle of winter, I knew I had the document somewhere, but we couldn't find it anywhere. The kids were freezing, so we just called another repairman and paid 220 GEL right away." (03)
- "When the repairman changed the part, he told me this looks like a factory defect and you were entitled to a free replacement. So I lost out twice just because of a lost piece of paper." (03)

## Pattern 2

**Pattern:** Documents end up in many places, filed by different people in different ways, so nobody can find or confirm one when it is needed.

**Interviews behind it:** 03, 04

**Quotes:**

- "I made folders on Google Drive broken down by month, but it's still chaos — people name files 'IMG_2041' and then I spend hours searching which receipt belongs to which invoice." (04)
- "When I have to submit tax declarations at the end of the month, I have to beg employees one by one on WhatsApp to send photos of expense receipts. Half are lost, half are blurry photos where numbers aren't visible." (04)
