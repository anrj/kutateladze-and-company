## The strongest incumbent

Paper kept in a folder or binder. It appears in both document logs (03, 04), and each interviewee still relies on the paper original: the homeowner keeps it in a hallway folder (03), and the accountant keeps a binder because the tax office wanted the physical slip (04). Both lost money when the paper went missing (220 GEL in 03, a 500 GEL fine in 04), yet neither gave up on it.
